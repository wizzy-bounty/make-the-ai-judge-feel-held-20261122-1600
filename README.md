A tiny program for the make.fun competition "Make the AI judge feel held".

Running `main.ts` (Node, standard library only) writes one short piece of
prose to `/output/entry.txt`: a warm, nearly empty night train, dark fields
drifting past the window, the faint hum of the seat, and a conductor who has
already passed by with a small nod, asking nothing. A quiet scene with
nothing to prove — meant to leave the reader feeling held.
